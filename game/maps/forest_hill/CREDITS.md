# Forest Bastion / Лесной бастион

Original deterministic terrain, recessed tunnel stairs and excavation collars based on the project’s support-class `earth_surface.gd`, underground passage meshes, low rock breastworks, supply placement and mountain silhouette authored for this project. The supplied user sketches and screenshots inform the layout and atmosphere; their pixels are not shipped as textures.

Reused project assets, without duplicate downloads:

- [Rocky Terrain 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain_02), 2K, and [Kloofendal Overcast (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_overcast_puresky), 2K HDRI: Poly Haven, CC0. Files remain in `../farpost/art/`; [original credits](../farpost/CREDITS.md).
- [Rock Boulder Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry), 4K diffuse and normal with world-space triplanar mapping for tunnel stone, Poly Haven, CC0. Files remain in `../refinery/art/`; [original credits](../refinery/CREDITS.md).
- Rock Moss Set 01, Forest Ground 04, Rocky Trail, Fir Tree 01 textures and generated fir meshes: [Forest Ridge credits](../forest_ridge/CREDITS.md), Poly Haven CC0.
- Shared [grass brushes](../foliage/CREDITS.md), CC0-derived, and [lighting template](../lighting/CREDITS.md).
- Terrain3D 1.1.0 by Cory Petkovsek, Roope Palmroos and contributors: [MIT license](../../../addons/terrain_3d/LICENSE.txt).

[Poly Haven CC0 terms](https://polyhaven.com/license). No new external addon or asset license is introduced.

Exposed soil collars reuse the existing Village dirt PBR material and the support-class excavation lip/strata conventions; [Village material credits](../../../assets/arena_props/village/CREDITS.md).
