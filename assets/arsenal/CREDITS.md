# Arsenal assets

The four weapon models, three grenade models, and vest pickup are original game assets. The metal and walnut use 2048-pixel color and normal maps from Poly Haven, both CC0. The vest reuses the [operator's fabric textures](../operator/CREDITS.md).

- [Metal Plate 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate_02)
- [American Walnut Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/american_walnut_veneer)

The 48 kHz mono footsteps and blast effects use Joseph Sardin's CC0 recordings. The flash is a short blast edit with a generated ring. The smoke hiss is generated. Weapon reports are mixes of the blast and [existing weapon recordings](../weapon_audio/CREDITS.md), including [the AK-47 report](../ak47/CREDITS.md).

- [Footsteps, Shoe on Concrete](https://bigsoundbank.com/footsteps-shoe-on-concrete-s0514.html)
- [Explosion #2](https://bigsoundbank.com/explosion-2-s1808.html)

Metal Plate 02's packed ambient-occlusion and roughness channels also supply surface detail on the M1, launcher, and TEC-9.
