# GPMG surface finishes

CC0 1.0 materials from Poly Haven, downloaded 22 November 2026:

- [Metal Plate 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate_02), Rob Tuytel: 4K diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness and metalness JPG maps. Model-space projection, dark steel / olive tints and reduced normal strength adapt the surface to the gun.
- [Dark Wood](https://polyhaven.com/a/dark_wood), Dimitrios Savva (photography), Dario Barresi (baking), Rico Cilliers (tiling): 4K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness JPG maps for the wooden stock and grip.
- [Studio Small 09](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09), Sergej Majboroda: existing `assets/operator/textures/hd/studio_small_09_4k.hdr`, reused for a subtle first-person metal reflection pass and studio review. Arena lighting remains active.
