# Objective props

The flag, charge, and backpack are built in Godot. The [flag reference by Vinny Passmore](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/flag-with-pole-e7bd2c1fb6af4b2a824cdd8ac9bcf6ec) and the supplied first-person charge screenshot informed the silhouettes.

- [Hessian 380](https://polyhaven.com/a/hessian_380), by Rico Cilliers and colormass, provides the CC0 fabric normal map for the flag and backpack.
- [Metal Plate 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate_02) provides CC0 steel color and normal maps for the charge, flag hardware, backpack fittings, and new arena cover.
- [Rusty Metal 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/rusty_metal_03) provides CC0 wear for the zone paint. The arenas reuse their existing Poly Haven environment lighting and textures.
