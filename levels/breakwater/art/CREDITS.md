# Breakwater 9 asset sources

The game uses processed copies of the following [Poly Haven CC0 assets](https://polyhaven.com/license).

- [Asphalt 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_01)
- [Concrete Floor Worn 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_001)
- [Container Side](https://polyhaven.com/a/container_side)
- [Metal Grate Rusty](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_grate_rusty)
- [Rusty Metal Sheet](https://polyhaven.com/a/rusty_metal_sheet)
- [Rusty Metal 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/rusty_metal_03)
- [Concrete Road Barrier](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_road_barrier)
- [Concrete Road Barrier 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_road_barrier_02)
- [Wooden Crate 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_crate_01)
- [Plastic Crate 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/plastic_crate_02)
- [Barrel 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/barrel_03)
- [Modular Industrial Pipes 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/modular_industrial_pipes_01)
- [Overhead Crane](https://polyhaven.com/a/overhead_crane)

Painted steel materials come from [ambientCG, also under CC0](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/).

- [Painted Metal 006](https://ambientcg.com/a/PaintedMetal006)
- [Painted Metal 008](https://ambientcg.com/a/PaintedMetal008)
- [Painted Metal 010](https://ambientcg.com/a/PaintedMetal010)

The crane, crates, and barriers were simplified for real-time rendering. The turbine pipework uses a subset of the source pipe kit. Surface maps use OpenGL normals and packed occlusion, roughness, and metallic channels. Container steel, Painted Metal 006, and Painted Metal 010 were desaturated for arena-specific colors. The hazard material combines the original arena's stripes with Rusty Metal 03.

## Turbine hall and freight yards

- [Modular Factory Facade](https://polyhaven.com/a/modular_factory_facade) supplies the clerestory frames and roller-door modules.
- [Hanging Industrial Lamp](https://polyhaven.com/a/hanging_industrial_lamp) supplies the hall pendants.
- [Industrial Caged Sconce](https://polyhaven.com/a/industrial_caged_sconce) supplies the portal fixtures.
- [Utility Box 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/utility_box_02) supplies the wall-mounted switchgear.
- [Freight Station](https://polyhaven.com/a/freight_station) supplies the HDR sky.

The turbine assemblies and corrugated shipping-container kit were modeled for Breakwater 9. Their weathered paint uses the processed PBR maps listed above.
